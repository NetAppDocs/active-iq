=== E-Series and StorageGRID support in Value Insights

Value Insights now includes ONTAP, E-Series, and StorageGRID systems in health, risk, security risk, and lifecycle totals. You can filter the dashboard by platform to view platform-specific details. Delivered and projected savings remain available for ONTAP systems only.
